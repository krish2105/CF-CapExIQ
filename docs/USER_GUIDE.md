# CapExIQ — User & Executive Operating Guide

## Executive Roles & Information Emphasis

- **CEO View**: Focuses on strategic-fit scores, value created, principal risks, board memorandum, customer impact.
- **CFO View**: Focuses on NPV, IRR, MIRR, PI, Payback, WACC, liquidity headroom, capital rationing, financial controls.
- **COO View**: Focuses on throughput capacity (orders/hr), labor productivity, facility utilization, downtime risk.
- **CTO View**: Focuses on technology architecture, integration dependencies, cybersecurity ratings, AI governance status.
- **Capital Committee View**: Focuses on decision pack, financial & strategic scoring, approval conditions, signed snapshot.
- **Analyst View**: Focuses on detailed assumptions register, formula inspector, cash flow schedule, data quality logs.

## Keyboard Shortcuts
- `Cmd + K` or `Ctrl + K`: Open Global Command Palette to jump to any page, switch scenario, or change theme.
