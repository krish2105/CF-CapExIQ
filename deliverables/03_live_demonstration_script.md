# CapExIQ: Live Application Demonstration Script

**Target Audience:** Professor & Capital Expenditure Committee  
**Total Demo Time:** 5 Minutes  

---

## Step 1: Landing Page & Project Context (0:00 – 0:45)
- Open browser to `http://localhost:3000/`.
- Point out **NovaRetail GCC** hypothetical entity branding and academic context notice.
- Highlight the **AED 24.0M** initial outlay and 4 strategic decision options (*Approve, Phased, Delay, Reject*).

## Step 2: Executive Dashboard (0:45 – 1:45)
- Navigate to `/dashboard`.
- Show top KPI cards: **NPV (AED 12.08M)**, **IRR (26.3%)**, **MIRR (19.3%)**, **PI (1.50x)**, **Payback (3.1 Years)**.
- Demonstrate interactive Recharts cash flow schedule and cumulative payback line chart crossing year 3.
- Highlight the **AI Executive Advisory Panel** with human review disclaimer.

## Step 3: Assumptions Register & Real-Time Recalculation (1:45 – 2:45)
- Navigate to `/assumptions`.
- Explain data classifications (*Historical, Current External, Forecast, User-entered*).
- Demonstrate real-time model updating: change **Automation Equipment** from `18,000,000` to `20,000,000`.
- Click **Save Updated Assumptions** and show instant recalculation across the application.

## Step 4: Scenario Engine & Downside Stress Testing (2:45 – 3:30)
- Navigate to `/scenarios`.
- Show side-by-side comparison between **Optimistic (NPV: 15.4M)**, **Base (NPV: 9.2M)**, and **Stress-Test Pessimistic (-2.1M)**.
- Use interactive sliders to adjust custom scenario multipliers live.

## Step 5: Sensitivity Matrix & Tornado Ranking (3:30 – 4:15)
- Navigate to `/sensitivity`.
- Showcase the **Tornado Chart** ranking Year-1 Operating Benefits as the #1 value driver.
- Toggle to **2-Way Heatmaps** to demonstrate NPV sensitivity across WACC and capex variations.

## Step 6: AI Assistant & Export (4:15 – 5:00)
- Navigate to `/ai-assistant`.
- Click suggested prompt chip: *"Why is MIRR lower than IRR?"* to show deterministic context injection.
- Click **Generate Board Memo** to produce executive memorandum.
- Go to `/financial-model` and click **Export Schedule to CSV** to complete demo.
