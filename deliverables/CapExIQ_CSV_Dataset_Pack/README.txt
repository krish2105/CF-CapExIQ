CapExIQ CSV Dataset Pack
========================

Contents
--------
01_dataco_operational_sample_20.csv
    Genuine sample rows from DataCo SMART SUPPLY CHAIN, cleaned to remove direct customer PII.
    This is a development sample, not the full 91.5 MB dataset.

02_monthly_operational_summary_sample.csv
    Aggregation derived from the real sample.

03_capexiq_project_assumptions.csv
    Hypothetical academic assumptions for NovaRetail GCC. These are not historical facts.

04_scenario_definitions.csv
    Optimistic, base and pessimistic scenario multipliers.

05_dewa_electricity_tariffs_july_2026.csv
    Official DEWA tariff slabs and July 2026 fuel surcharge.

06_uae_corporate_tax_rates.csv
    Official UAE headline corporate-tax bands.

07_cbuae_eibor_download_catalog.csv
    Official source catalogue for EIBOR history. Rate observations are not reproduced here.

08_data_dictionary.csv
    Definitions of important fields.

09_data_source_register.csv
    Source, licence/status and use guidance.

10_finance_formula_catalog.csv
    Model formula reference.

Important use rules
-------------------
- Do not claim the DataCo monetary amounts are UAE/AED values.
- Do not claim NovaRetail assumptions are real historical company data.
- Use deterministic code for NPV/IRR; use AI only to explain validated outputs.
- Replace major project assumptions with vendor quotations where possible.
- The full DataCo file should be downloaded from the official Mendeley page listed in the source register.
